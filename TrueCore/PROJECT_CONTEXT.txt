PROJECT: TrueCore Packet Assistant

Purpose:
TrueCore reviews patient packet files, extracts structured fields, validates packet readiness,
scores packet quality, and presents operator guidance through the desktop GUI and launcher.

Core Runtime Areas:
- TrueCore/
  Application runtime, GUI, launcher, support utilities, and host-side orchestration.
- TrueCoreIntel/
  Extraction, validation, intelligence, review, and scoring layers.

Key Product Surfaces:
- Launcher
  Secure launch flow, update checks, office snapshot support, and support actions.
- Main GUI
  Packet intake, analysis results, packet details, math view, admin overview, office comparison,
  operations, and audit views.
- Admin
  Local business intelligence, office comparison snapshots, operational controls, and audit logs.

Data and Runtime Notes:
- Local writable runtime data belongs in the machine-level TrueCore data directory.
- Packet history, office snapshots, and support outputs are local-first unless explicitly exported.
- De-identified snapshot export/import is the supported office-comparison workflow.

Project Documentation Files:
- PROJECT_CONTEXT.txt
  High-level architecture and product map.
- PROJECT_STATE.txt
  Current system status and capability notes.
- DEV_NOTES.txt
  Development guidance and implementation notes.

Engineering Rules:
- Make the smallest safe change possible when editing source.
- Preserve working import structure and pipeline order.
- Keep operator-facing wording in plain English.
- Verify behavior after edits instead of assuming the change is harmless.
